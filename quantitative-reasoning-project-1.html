Project Scenarios

Choose one of the following scenarios:

Scenario A

In 1999 NASA lost the $125 million Mars Climate Orbiter because engineers failed to convert between English and metric units.

Source: LA Times

Scenario B

Air Canada’s Flight 143 (1983) ran out of fuel mid-flight due to a miscalculation mixing metric and imperial units.

Source: Wikipedia

Scenario C

In 1999, a Korean Air cargo jet crashed after the crew misinterpreted an altitude instruction of 1,500 meters as 1,500 feet (assuming the usual unit was feet).

Source: NIST

All scenarios are designed to address the same underlying learning goals. Your work will be graded using the same rubric, regardless of which scenario you choose.

Quantities, units, and assumptions

Topic-Specific Guidance

Each scenario in this project describes the same type of mistake in quantitative reasoning. For whichever scenario you choose, describe that mistake as thoroughly as possible.

For example, for Scenario C, you should consider the difference between 1500 meters and 1500 feet. See the rubric in the Project Guidelines for additional guidance.
