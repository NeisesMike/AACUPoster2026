Project Scenarios

Choose one of the following scenarios:

Cell phone plans

Bialasik & Martin (2011) compared three phone plans (different base fees and per-text charges) by graphing linear cost equations to find which plan is cheapest at various usage levels.

Source: Bialasik & Martin (2011)

Wichita transit

A monthly bus pass in Wichita costs $25 and a single ride $1.75, so a commuter breaks even after ~15 rides (25/1.75).

Source: Not provided

Travel cost

For a ~200-mile trip from Wichita to Kansas City, driving (~$30 fuel) vs a Greyhound bus ticket (~$30) have roughly equal cost, illustrating a linear cost comparison under different travel options.

Sources: Source 1; Source 2

All scenarios are designed to address the same underlying learning goals. Your work will be graded using the same rubric, regardless of which scenario you choose.

Linear relationships as comparisons

Topic-Specific Guidance

For each scenario, your project should at least:

  • Contain a relevant graph (or graphs) that help to visualize the topic matter.
  • Contain an explanation of the graph (or graphs) that clarify what each part of the graph means: slope, y-intercept, x-intercept, and any intersection point (if one exists).
  • Contain a reflection over the results of your quantitative analysis. If none of these scenarios is very applicable to you, please indicate a related scenario that is more applicable!
